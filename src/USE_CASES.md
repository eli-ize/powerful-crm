## 🎯 How to Use This Platform for ANY Industry

### Step 1: Choose Your Industry
Use the **Industry Templates** feature to select a pre-built template or create custom criteria.

### Step 2: Find Leads
Use **Lead Finder** to discover businesses that match your target profile:
- Google Places API: Find local businesses
- Custom searches: "restaurants in Miami", "law firms in NYC"
- Filter by: Industry, location, size, ratings

### Step 3: Qualify Leads
Create **Qualification Categories** based on pain points:
- What problem do you solve?
- Who has that problem?
- How can you identify them?

### Step 4: Create Campaign
Design your **AI Calling Campaign**:
- Write compelling scripts
- Set qualification criteria
- Define next actions (book meeting, send proposal, etc.)
- Configure AI voice and tone

### Step 5: Deploy Virtual Agents
Let **AI Agents** do the work:
- Make calls 24/7
- Qualify prospects automatically
- Book meetings in your calendar
- Update CRM in real-time
- Generate call insights

### Step 6: Close Deals
Focus on **high-quality leads**:
- AI pre-qualifies prospects
- You get warm, interested leads
- Close faster, higher conversion
- Scale without hiring

## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. **Niche Down**
Don't try to sell to everyone. Pick ONE industry and dominate it.

### 2. **Solve Real Pain**
Your qualification criteria should identify URGENT problems, not nice-to-haves.

### 3. **Perfect Your Script**
Test different approaches. A/B test scripts. Optimize based on data.

### 4. **Follow Up Fast**
Speed to lead matters. AI agents can respond in seconds.

### 5. **Track Everything**
Use analytics to see what works. Double down on winners.
